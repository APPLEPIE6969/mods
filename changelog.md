# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2025-01-13

### Added
- **50+ Drug Items**: Comprehensive collection including stimulants, depressants, psychedelics, solvents, and synthetic opioids
- **Specialized Crafting Tables**: 
  - Chemistry Lab Table (Meth, LSD synthesis)
  - Drug Processing Table (Cocaine, Heroin, Crack)
  - Extraction Table (DMT, Mescaline, Kratom)
  - Synthesis Table (Ecstasy, Fentanyl, GHB)
- **Realistic Effects System**: Proper drug effects with accurate durations and intensities
- **Dangerous Combination System**: Fatal combinations with warning messages
- **World Generation**: Natural drug plants spawning in appropriate biomes
- **Loot Distribution**: Chemicals and equipment in various structures
- **Ingredient System**: 30+ chemical ingredients and lab equipment items
- **Overdose Mechanics**: Health loss and naloxone treatment
- **Withdrawal Effects**: Anxiety, paranoia, psychosis
- **16x16 Textures**: Custom textures for all items

### Features
- **Chemical Accuracy**: Realistic synthesis recipes based on actual drug chemistry
- **Safety Warnings**: In-game warnings for dangerous drug combinations
- **Educational Value**: Accurate information about drug effects and risks
- **Balanced Gameplay**: Proper difficulty progression for obtaining ingredients

### Content Warning
- Mature themes including drug use, addiction, and overdose
- Intended for mature audiences only
- Educational disclaimer included
